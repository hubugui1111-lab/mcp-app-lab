# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a technical community where people can question assumptions, reproduce failures, and learn without harassment. Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Welcome different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and communication styles.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- focusing critique on code, behavior, and evidence;
- explaining reproduction steps and uncertainty;
- accepting corrections and crediting contributors;
- protecting private data disclosed during debugging;
- helping keep discussions on topic.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or retaliation for a good-faith report.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily limit participation, or permanently ban a participant when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Enforcement decisions may be appealed once with new relevant context.

## Attribution
